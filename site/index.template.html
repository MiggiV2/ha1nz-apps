The badge opens your F-Droid client and adds this repository, so the app updates itself from then on. The APK button downloads the arm64-v8a build straight from here, no client needed.

How this repo works. Apps here are AI-authored on purpose. A human acts as product owner: choosing what to build, reviewing the code, manually testing each release, and signing & publishing it. Full source is linked per app — audit it yourself.
