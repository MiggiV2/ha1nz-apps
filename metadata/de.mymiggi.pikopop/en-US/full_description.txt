A Matrix messenger for people who talk in stickers. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose,
Material 3, and the matrix-rust-sdk — the same crypto library Element uses.
The interface is German.

Features:

* Sign in through MAS/OIDC in a Custom Tab with PKCE — the app never renders a password field at all
* Every room in one list: unread counts, mute state, encryption state, last message, filters for unread and Spaces, pending invitations
* Timeline with replies, edits, deletions, reactions, typing indicators and read receipts, images from the photo picker or the camera — and no camera and no storage permission is declared
* Stickers first class: a bookshelf of your packs, a picker that stays out of the way, sending as m.sticker rather than as an image
* Import a FluffyChat sticker pack from a zip, or a pack another account published — MSC2545 in both its stable and its unstable spelling
* Encryption you can actually see: one Security screen for session verification, cross-signing, key backup and the recovery key, each with its own honest state
* An undecryptable message is not a dead end — it names the cause and offers exactly the remedy that applies
* Push through UnifiedPush, so no Google Play Services are needed
* Four themes, each with a light and a dark palette, and a brightness override independent of the system
* Privacy first — no trackers, no ads, no analytics; the session is encrypted at rest and excluded from cloud backup

The app talks to the homeserver you sign in to, and to nothing else. There is
no account with the developer, no telemetry and no crash reporting.

This is a pre-release: it works, it is used daily, and interoperability with
Element and FluffyChat is tested by hand in both directions — but not every
corner has seen a device yet.

This app is developed with AI (Claude / Claude Code) and manually tested and
maintained by a human.
