New

- Send files: in the attachment sheet, "Datei" sends any document, such as a
  PDF, a spreadsheet or an archive. A dialog first shows its name, its size
  and an optional description. The file goes out unchanged, under its own
  name.
- Save files: a received file is a row of its own with name and size. A tap
  saves it decrypted to Download/Piko Pop, and "Öffnen" in the message that
  follows opens it straight away in a matching app.
- A background per chat: in the room details, "Chat-Hintergrund" gives each
  chat a pattern or photo of its own. "Standard verwenden" goes back to the
  general background.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
