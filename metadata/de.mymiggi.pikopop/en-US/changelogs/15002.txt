New

- Share location: "Standort" in the attachment sheet sends where you are.
  Received locations open in the maps app.
- Live location: share in the background for a chosen time, shown as a card
  in the room with distance and "Beenden". Only in rooms that allow it.
- Polls: view, vote, end and create them.
- Favourites sit at the top of the room list.
- Gallery: send photos and videos together in one dialog, with thumbnails.
- Deleted messages collapse into one plain line.

Bug fixes

- A reply loads its quote again instead of giving up.
- With location switched off, the dialog says so and offers "Standort
  einschalten".
- The blurred preview in the send dialog is really blurred.
- Videos without a reported type are recognised as videos.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
