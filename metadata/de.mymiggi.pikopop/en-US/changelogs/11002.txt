New features

- A video from a chat can be saved to the gallery. "Save" is now offered on a
  video too, both in the action sheet and in the fullscreen player. The video is
  never loaded into memory as a whole: it is decrypted into the cache and
  streamed from there into Movies/Piko Pop.

Bug fixes

- Notifications stay put. Since 0.1.9 an incoming message could empty the whole
  shade: Android withdraws every notification on a channel it deletes, and the
  channel upkeep ran on a room list that reports itself empty once at the start
  of every session. Only channels that no longer hold a notification are deleted
  now.
- A cold start leaves other notifications alone. If the app had been killed and
  was then woken by a message, the fresh process cancelled the summary the old
  one had posted, which took every notification under it along. A summary is now
  only cancelled by the process that posted it. Measured on the device: four
  notifications stay standing where previously only the newest one survived.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
