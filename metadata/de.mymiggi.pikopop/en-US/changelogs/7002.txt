Four things, all prompted by a pass on the device.

A notification that had been swiped away came back. The plan re-posted every
chat it held on every update, and a swipe was not modelled at all — so a
message in any other chat resurrected the one just dismissed. A delete intent
now reports the swipe, the chat drops out of the store, and only a chat whose
content changed is posted again. Every change also carries a sequence number,
so an overtaken update cannot re-post a chat that was just swiped away. The
"Gelesen" action is gone; reading in the chat still clears it, which was the
part that worked.

While the app is open, other people see "online". The SDK can only write
presence, so a call of our own reads everyone else's — every 60 seconds in the
foreground, at most 20 people per round. A small dot on the avatar, in the chat
list and in the room header: filled for online, a ring for away, nothing for
offline or unknown. A homeserver with presence switched off answers "offline"
for everybody, so that answer is read as unknown rather than painting someone
offline who is sitting in another client. And the "offline" on leaving is best
effort: if the process is frozen first, the session keeps reading as online
until the server's own timeout.

A photo opens fullscreen on a tap, where it can be zoomed and panned. Tapping
anywhere else in the bubble still opens the message actions.

In the room info, "Speichern" is a real button instead of a word in empty
space, and the room header no longer clips the first character off the trust
line.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
