This release collects everything added since 0.1.4: 0.1.5 and 0.1.6 never made
it into the repo.

Video, end to end: a received video plays in the chat with a poster frame,
pause, seeking and a way out; your own videos go out with a poster frame and a
size check; recording works inside the app without a camera permission; and a
video is compressed before it is sent, 720p or 1080p, and the choice is
remembered.

Photos and videos leave the device without metadata. A JPEG loses EXIF, GPS,
XMP and the device identifier, an MP4 loses the location, the Android version
and the timestamps, without a single byte moving. WebM and MKV cannot be
cleaned and are refused rather than sent quietly. The file name no longer says
anything about the camera.

New: a photo can be sent veiled, one photo at a time. The switch sits in the
send dialog rather than in the settings, so it applies to that one picture. The
photo stays unrecognisable for you as well until it is tapped, and other
clients receive a deliberately destroyed thumbnail.

Message text can now be copied from the action sheet, offered only where there
is text.

Also from 0.1.5 and 0.1.6: links in messages are tappable, an image is sent from
a dialog with a description as one message,
photos open fullscreen and can be zoomed, room details list the participants
with their Matrix ids and let you change name and topic, sharing shows the chat
list with a search, read chats leave the notification shade and swiped ones
stay gone, you can see who is online, the chrome has its own icon set instead
of emoji, and a homeserver without MAS can be signed into with a password.

Honestly: the video features and the veil have not yet been exercised in every
corner on a device.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
