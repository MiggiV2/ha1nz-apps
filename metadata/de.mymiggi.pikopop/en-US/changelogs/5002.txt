Two new features: voice messages and image compression.

Voice messages can now be recorded, previewed, discarded and sent, and an
incoming one is drawn as its own item with play, duration, progress and a
waveform instead of an unsupported message. Whether something is a voice
message is decided by the MSC3245 marker alone; a plain audio file stays an
attachment. Recording produces Ogg with Opus from Android 10 on, and it is sent
with a measured duration and a measured waveform. Verified in both directions
against FluffyChat. This adds the microphone permission, which is asked for at
the first recording rather than at start-up, and a refusal is explained instead
of retried.

Images are no longer sent straight away. They are shown first, with a toggle
for compression and the real measured sizes before and after. Compression
targets WebP at quality 80 with a longest edge of 2048 pixels, which usually
takes a camera photo down by more than 90 percent. The choice is remembered.
Animated images and real transparency go out untouched, and re-encoding drops
every EXIF tag, including location and device identifiers; only the orientation
survives, because it is baked into the pixels.

One bug less as well: the note about a skipped animation belongs to stickers
and no longer shows up on ordinary images.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
