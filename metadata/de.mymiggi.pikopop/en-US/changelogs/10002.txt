New features

- Every chat gets its own ringtone and is listed under "Conversations" in the
  system settings rather than under notification categories.
- Swiping a message to the right replies to it.
- Voice messages play at 0.75x to 2x.
- After the first sign-in a walkthrough leads to working push: permission,
  UnifiedPush distributor, ntfy, battery optimisation.
- Your own profile picture is shown and can be changed or removed.
- Up to ten photos can be picked and sent at once.
- Stickers leave the workshop as WebP only; the format choice is gone.

Bug fixes

- Coming back from the browser lands in the running app instead of a second
  Piko Pop with the tab left open. The browser is handed the app's brightness,
  and a sign-in waiting on it can be called off.
- The sticker workshop scrolls when the keyboard comes up, so the shortcode
  field stays visible.
- Still stickers no longer report a skipped animation.

Documentation

- The device pass for the conversation channels is written up, MANUAL-TEST.md
  is gone.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
