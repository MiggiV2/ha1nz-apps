Two things about video.

A video is now measured by what will actually be uploaded rather than by the raw
file. What gets checked is the estimated size after compression, target bitrate
plus audio times length. The raw file used to be the one checked: a 12 second
recording of 63 MB was discarded with "the server accepts at most 50 MB",
although 720p would have turned it into about 4 MB. Only what would still be too
large after compression is refused now, and the reason says how large it would
have stayed. The camera caps a recording by length rather than by bytes, so it
no longer cuts off mid scene.

Sending a video now asks first. The dialog names the room, the file name, the
length, the size and the estimated size after compression, and it takes a
description. Only "Senden" works out the plan and starts the encoder.
Cancelling, a second recording and leaving the room delete the video and its
poster frame from the cache.

New: a video can be sent veiled, the way a photo already could. The switch sits
in the send dialog and is off every time, so it applies to that one video. What
goes out with the event as its preview is a deliberately destroyed poster frame.
A video has no blurhash, so that frame is the only preview there is. A veiled
video shows no play badge; the first tap reveals it, the second opens the player,
and the player is never veiled.

Fixed along the way: a recording made in the app arrived as "…-kamera.mp4" on
the other side. The file name no longer says where it came from.
