New

- Media overview: in the room details, "Medien" shows every photo and video
  of a chat as a grid, grouped by month. A tap opens it in the usual
  fullscreen viewer.
- Chat background: under Appearance, every chat can have a pattern in a
  colour or a photo of your own behind it, with brightness and blur. Confetti
  is the default; "Keiner" turns it off.
- Hide unknown events: what Piko Pop cannot display, such as polls or calls,
  no longer shows up as an empty line in the timeline. A switch under
  Appearance brings it back.

Bug fixes

- Your first message of a new day stays in view. When it arrived together
  with its day divider, the timeline stayed on the previous message.
- Notification rules, the device list, presence and sticker packs no longer
  fail with 401 when an OIDC server's short-lived access token has just
  expired. Piko Pop renews it and asks again.

Other

- Matrix SDK 26.09.26 and current Android libraries.

Signed with our own key. This APK is for arm64-v8a.
