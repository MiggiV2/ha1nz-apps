Current prices for E5, E10 and diesel at German filling stations — fetched quickly, with nobody looking over your shoulder.

<b>What Tankblick does</b>

* Search nearby, with price, distance and open/closed status
* Favourites for the stations you actually visit — all refreshed in a single request
* Detail view with all three prices, the address and opening hours
* Navigation with the app of your choice: Organic Maps, OsmAnd, or whatever else you have installed
* Keeps working offline, and says honestly how old the prices on screen are

<b>What Tankblick does not do</b>

No account. No tracking, no analytics, no ads. No background requests.

The app talks to exactly one server: creativecommons.tankerkoenig.de. Your location leaves the device only as a coordinate pair in a search you started yourself. Favourites, settings and prices stay local, and price history is deleted after 30 days.

<b>You need your own API key</b>

Tankerkönig issues keys per person, and a key hidden inside the app could be extracted anyway. Register for free at onboarding.tankerkoenig.de — first name, last name and email address are required. Keys are reviewed by hand, which can take a few days. A demo key is available in the app for trying it out.

Your key is encrypted with AES-256-GCM in the Android Keystore and excluded from cloud backup.
